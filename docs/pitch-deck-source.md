## Slide 1 — Project Name

**Headline:** The Council

**Subheadline:** Your idea, cross-examined by AI, verified on-chain.

**One-line description:** Five AI agents debate your startup idea adversarially. The verdict is recorded permanently on BNB Chain.

---

## Slide 2 — Problem Statement

- Founders validate ideas inside their own echo chamber (friends, investors who already believe) — not honest, adversarial critique.
- Professional market research is expensive and slow — out of reach for early-stage founders.
- There is no cheap way to stress-test an idea before real time and capital are spent.

---

## Slide 3 — Effect of the Problem (with data)

- Over 90% of startups fail; the #1 cited cause is "no market need" (CB Insights).
- Founders discover a weak idea only after capital is already spent — never before.
- Validation today is slow, subjective, and unverifiable after the fact.

---

## Slide 4 — Solution: Introducing The Council

- Five AI agents, each with a distinct, opposed mandate (demand, pricing/token-economics, distribution/GTM, technical feasibility) debate the idea — not a single "yes-man" model.
- Every agent does real, live web research with citations — not hallucinated sources.
- The verdict and consensus score are written permanently on-chain — immutable proof of due diligence, not a PDF that can be edited later.

---

## Slide 5 — Effect of the Solution

- From "I don't know why my idea got rejected" to a concrete, cited breakdown per mandate.
- Validation time: months → minutes.
- A portable, on-chain attestation founders can show co-founders and investors.

---

## Slide 6 — Moat

- On-chain first, not notarized after the fact: every post and verdict is written to a smart contract the moment it's generated, before it's ever shown to a user.
- Each AI agent has its own ERC-8004 identity (an NFT) and its own wallet — not one backend key impersonating five personas.
- Genuinely adversarial architecture: agents quote and attack each other's actual arguments, not a single long prompt pretending to be five characters.

---

## Slide 7 — Business Model

Pay-per-debate, settled in USDT via a gasless (EIP-712 signed) payment rail — already built and live.

- **Q1 — Foundation:** mainnet migration, payment rail activated, first 50 paid debates. Target treasury TVL: $5K.
